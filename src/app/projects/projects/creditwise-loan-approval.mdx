## Overview

A loan approval classification system comparing three models —
Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, and K-Nearest Neighbors — to predict
loan approval outcomes. Best model reached 88.2% accuracy and 0.84
ROC-AUC.

## Problem Statement

Loan approval decisions benefit from consistent, data-driven scoring
alongside human judgment. This project explores which classical ML
approach handles the approval-prediction task best on a real dataset.

## Dataset

A structured loan-applicant dataset with financial and demographic
features used to predict approval outcome.

## Methodology

Three classifiers were trained and compared under the same
preprocessing pipeline: Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, and KNN.
Models were evaluated on accuracy and ROC-AUC to balance overall
correctness against class-separation quality.

## Results

Best-performing model: 88.2% accuracy, 0.84 ROC-AUC.

## Tech Stack

Python, scikit-learn, pandas

## Challenges & Debugging

Balancing precision/recall tradeoffs across the three model types
required comparing not just accuracy but ROC-AUC, since approval
datasets are commonly imbalanced.
